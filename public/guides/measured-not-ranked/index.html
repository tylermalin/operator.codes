Measured, not ranked. How AI answer engines choose what to cite.

−39.8%

Organic clicks lost where Google shows an AI Overview, from a randomized experiment on 1,065 users. Satisfaction did not change.

What the evidence says moves a citation, what wastes the budget, and a dated 90-day plan.

Executive summary

00

Lead with the recommendation, then the risks

Treat AI search as SEO plus reputation plus verification.

Make sure the AI crawlers can reach you. Make your most exposed pages the clearest, best-sourced statement of their facts. Get referenced by credible third parties. Then measure how often you show up across many prompts, because a single "AI rank" is mostly noise.

Risks first

  • Most tactics on the market have no evidence behind them. Server logs show 97% of llms.txt files get no requests from AI crawlers, and the one controlled test of adding schema found no citation lift.
  • Manipulation is already happening and is being treated as an attack. Microsoft found 31 companies hiding prompt injections in "Summarize with AI" buttons.
  • Run the same brand-recommendation prompt twice and the two lists match less than once in 100 tries.
  • Where an AI Overview appears, organic clicks drop by roughly 40%.

Where it pays

  • Sourced claims can lift a lower-ranked page past the leader. In the founding GEO experiment, adding citations raised the fifth-ranked page's visibility 115% while the top page lost 30%.
  • Branded web mentions correlate with AI Overview visibility about three times as strongly as backlinks.
  • A brand cited inside an AI Overview gets 120% more organic clicks per impression than one left out.
  • Google's own guide says unique, first-hand content is the factor most likely to drive presence over time.

The shift

01

The answer moved above the link

The clicks that remained were not better clicks.

Agarwal (Indian School of Business) and Sen (Carnegie Mellon) used a Chrome extension to randomly hide AI Overviews for 1,065 US desktop users between January 7 and February 10, 2026. When an Overview showed, outbound organic clicks fell 39.8% and zero-click searches rose 34.5%. Sponsored clicks held flat.

Bounce rate, back-button returns and time on page matched across groups, which cuts against Google's claim that Overview clicks are higher quality. More than 95% of participants never noticed their results had changed.

Ahrefs says the damage doubled since April 2025. Seer saw a rebound in early 2026. Google disputed Pew's method. For planning, assume 40 to 60% fewer clicks on informational queries that trigger an Overview and treat any recovery as upside.

Demand is still growing. OpenAI reported 900 million weekly ChatGPT users in February 2026, up from 400 million a year earlier. Queries are getting longer too: Pew found 8% of one- or two-word searches produced an AI summary, against 53% of searches with ten or more words. People can now read and act on your content without visiting the page it came from.

How engines choose sources

02

Ranking became one input among several

Engines retrieve passages, write one answer, and cite what supports it.

Google's May 2026 guide says its AI features run on its core ranking systems and names two techniques: retrieval-augmented generation and query fan-out. Select a stage.

What the GEO experiment tested

03

The paper that named the field, read closely

Sourced claims beat keywords. Lower-ranked pages gained the most.

Aggarwal, Murahari and colleagues at Princeton, Georgia Tech, the Allen Institute and IIT Delhi built a 10,000-query benchmark, fed the top five Google results to a GPT-3.5 answer engine, rewrote one source at a time with nine methods, and measured how much of the answer each source earned.

Exhibit 5 · GEO-bench · position-adjusted word count

Visibility change by rewrite method

Read with care

  • It is a 2023 lab result on GPT-3.5 over five fixed sources. Production engines in 2026 retrieve far more widely.
  • The rewriting prompts allowed invented statistics and quotes, and the lab engine could not tell. Journalists, regulators and later models can. Use real data or none.
  • Effects vary by domain. Statistics helped most on law, government and opinion queries; quotations on people, society and history.

A citable passage

  • States one claim plainly near the top of its section, in a sentence that still makes sense when lifted out.
  • Carries a number, a date or a named source the model can attach to the claim.
  • Says something the model cannot produce on its own: first-party data, a measured result, a real decision and what followed.
  • Sits on a page that other credible sites also reference.

Evidence ledger

04

Every common tactic, graded

Squares show evidence strength, from one vendor correlation (one) to a controlled test or a platform statement backed by independent data (five).

Answer engines can also be gamed in ways classic search could not. They read retrieved text as context, and models follow instructions found in text. Researchers at USC and Arizona State showed hidden, fluent text in a product description can push that product up an LLM's ranking. Microsoft's Defender team found more than 50 hidden prompt injections from 31 real companies across 14 industries. Treat anything you would not want a journalist to find in your page source as off limits.

Crawler access policy

05

Search, live-fetch and training bots are separate switches

Blocking a search bot removes you from that engine's answers. Blocking a training bot does not. Set each one and copy the rules.


      

      
Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode are governed by Googlebot and snippet controls, so blocking Googlebot removes you from Search entirely. Check your CDN too: Cloudflare blocks AI crawlers by default on new domains, and since September 15, 2026 has blocked training and agent crawlers by default on ad-supported pages for new domains and free plans.

Measurement
06
Track presence across many runs

  

    
Position is noise. Frequency is the signal.

    

      
SparkToro and Gumshoe.ai ran the same recommendation prompts repeatedly through ChatGPT, Claude and Google's AI. The chance of getting an identical brand list twice was under 1 in 100. Track how often you appear across a fixed prompt set, the way a pollster tracks share across many interviews.

      
Search Console added a Generative AI report on June 3, 2026. It shows impressions in AI Overviews, AI Mode and Discover by page, country and device. It does not show clicks or the queries behind them.

How AI search programs go wrong

  

    
The expensive mistake is counting activity as progress.

Buying the checklistllms.txt, AI schema, chunked pages and a rank tracker sold as a packageHold each tactic to the evidence ledger. Demand a control group.

        
Fabricated evidenceStatistics and quotes added to look citable, without real sourcingEvery number traces to a source you can produce on request.

Thesis
09
Why the evidence lines up the way it does

  
An engine that answers in its own voice carries the risk of being wrong, so it favors sources it can safely repeat.

  

    

    

      
For twenty-five years search rewarded the page that best matched a query and collected the most links. The engine only pointed, and the user judged. Answer engines took on the judging. One academic measurement already finds about one claim in nine unsupported by the pages an Overview cites, and every operator has reason to close that gap.

      
Statistics and named sources lift visibility because they can be checked. Third-party mentions predict inclusion because they corroborate. First-hand content wins because the model does not already have it. Prompt injection is being catalogued as an attack because it corrupts the checking.

      
The missing layer in AI search is provenance: a reliable way for a machine to know where a claim came from, who stands behind it and whether it held up. Organizations that publish measured, sourced and corroborated facts are building that layer for their own category. At scale, the incentives now reward accuracy and shown work, which good institutions valued long before search existed.
