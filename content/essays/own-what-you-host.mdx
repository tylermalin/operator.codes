- The standard hyperscale data center deal: outside capital in,
  community gets a moratorium fight and jobs numbers that don't
  survive scrutiny.
- The real issue: governance and cash-flow rights are bundled together
  by default. They don't have to be.
- The e-corporation mechanism: separate the two. Outside capital gets
  a fair return and a profit share. Governance stays with the people
  who live next to the thing.
- Why it stalled the first time (gig platforms): uncertain cash flow
  plus a novel governance structure was too much risk at once for
  impact capital to underwrite.
- Why data centers fix that: predictable, contracted cash flow removes
  the underwriting objection. The governance innovation stands on its
  own now.
