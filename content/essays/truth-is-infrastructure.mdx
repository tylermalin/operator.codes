Most systems today rely on estimates. Carbon markets, ESG reporting, supply chains, increasingly AI energy disclosures.

The problem is not a lack of data. The problem is that very little of the data is cryptographically tied to reality at the point of measurement.

That gap becomes expensive at scale.
